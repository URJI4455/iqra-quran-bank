What We Do

Four Pillars of Impact

Every donation you make funds one of our four core programmes — each designed to address a specific need in rural Ethiopian Muslim communities.

Qur'an Bank

Thousands of children in rural Ethiopia are eager to learn the Qur'an but lack access to a single copy. We collect, distribute, and replace Qur'an copies for communities across Bale, Arsi, and Hararge.

2000+ Copies Distributed

Madrasa Construction

A Madrasa is more than a building — it is the foundation of a community's Islamic identity. We build simple, durable schools equipped with furniture, blackboards, and Qur'an copies for every student.

0 Madrasas Built

Solar Energy

In many rural villages, mosques and schools go dark at sunset. Solar panels extend learning hours, enable night prayers, and eliminate dangerous kerosene lanterns for the whole community.

0 Facilities Powered

Clean Water

Clean water is not just a health need — for Muslims, access to clean water is central to worship. We fund wells, pumps, and filtration systems in villages where families walk kilometres to find water.

0 Water Points Installed

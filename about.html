About Iqra Qur'an Bank

A registered Ethiopian charity founded on faith, built on transparency, and driven by the belief that every child deserves access to the Holy Qur'an.

Our Story

Born from a Belief That No Child Should Be Left Without the Qur'an

Iqra Qur'an Bank was founded in 2025 in Robe, Bale, Ethiopia — in the heart of a region where thousands of Muslim children have no access to a single copy of the Holy Qur'an.

Our founders witnessed firsthand how children in remote villages memorised the Qur'an from a single worn copy shared between dozens of students — or studied without one at all. They saw Madrasas operating under open skies with no shelter, no lights, and no clean water.

Iqra Qur'an Bank was created to bridge that gap — systematically, transparently, and with the documentation that donors worldwide deserve. Every project is photographed and video documented so every supporter knows exactly where their donation went.

Our Mission

To Ensure Every Muslim Child in Rural Ethiopia Has Access to the Holy Qur'an

We believe Islamic education is a fundamental right. Every child born into a Muslim family deserves a copy of the Qur'an, access to a Madrasa, and basic utilities that support learning and worship — regardless of how remote their village is.

Our Vision

One Million Qur'an Copies Distributed Across Ethiopia

Our long-term vision is to distribute one million Qur'an copies to underprivileged children across Ethiopia — and to build the educational and utility infrastructure that sustains Islamic learning for generations.

Our Work

Four Pillars of Our Programme

Each pillar addresses a different but equally critical dimension of community development.

Qur'an Bank

We collect donations to purchase and distribute Qur'an copies to children who cannot afford one. Each copy is delivered in person and documented with photos.

Madrasa Construction

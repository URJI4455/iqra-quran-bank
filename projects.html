Qur'an Bank

Distributing the Holy Qur'an

Thousands of children in rural Ethiopia memorise the Qur'an from a single worn copy shared between dozens of students — or study without one at all. The Qur'an Bank collects, purchases, and personally delivers Qur'an copies to children who cannot afford one.

Each delivery is photographed, and a short video is sent to the sponsoring donor within days of distribution. Every child. Every copy. Documented.

Madrasa Construction

Building Islamic Schools

Many villages across Bale and Arsi lack any dedicated space for Islamic education. Children sit outdoors in the heat or rain, learning from a single teacher with no materials, no chairs, and no shelter.

We build simple, durable Madrasas equipped with furniture, blackboards, and a full set of Qur'an copies — every student receives one on day one. Construction is photographed at every stage.

️ Solar Energy

Lighting Mosques and Schools

In rural Ethiopia, electricity is either absent or unaffordable for most mosques and Madrasas. When the sun sets, so does all learning and community activity. Dangerous kerosene lanterns are the only alternative.

We install complete solar systems — panels, batteries, and LED lights — enabling night prayers, evening study sessions, and safe community gatherings after dark.

Clean Water

Bringing Clean Water to Communities

In many remote villages, families — especially women and children — walk several kilometres each day to collect water from unsafe sources. Clean water is also essential for Wudu (ritual purification) before every prayer.

We fund hand-dug wells, motorised pumps, and filtration systems that bring clean, safe water directly into communities — improving health, enabling worship, and restoring dignity.

Information We Collect

Iqra Qur'an Bank collects personal information only when you voluntarily provide it to us — for example, when you make a donation, submit a contact form, or subscribe to our community updates.

The information we may collect includes your full name, email address, phone number, country of residence, and any message or communication you send us.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To process and acknowledge your donation and send you payment instructions.
  • To send you documentation (photos and videos) of how your donation was used.
  • To respond to enquiries you send via our contact form.
  • To send community updates and field reports if you have subscribed.
  • To maintain records for our legal and charitable compliance obligations.

Sharing Your Data

Iqra Qur'an Bank does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, or any commercial entity.

We may share limited information only where required by Ethiopian law or by lawful request from relevant authorities.

Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the personal information you provide. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We store your data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to request correction of inaccurate data, and to request deletion of your data where we have no legal obligation to retain it.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at iqraquranbank@gmail.com or by phone at 0984 617 171.
